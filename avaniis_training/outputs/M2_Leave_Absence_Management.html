Section 1
Module Overview & Preconditions
The Leave & Absence Management module in AVANIIS HR handles every employee absence type from request to payroll notification. Leave balances accrue monthly via a cron-driven engine, employees submit requests through self-service (/me/leave), managers approve within a 48-hour SLA, and the system automatically deducts balances, blocks the team calendar, and notifies the payroll run — all with an immutable audit trail for UAE Labour Law (FDL 33/2021) compliance.
✅ Preconditions Checklist — All must be true before any leave request can be submitted

✓
Leave approval workflow configured
Configure at: /settings/workflows
🚫 Blocking Conditions — System will refuse the leave request
No leave balanceabsence_balances.remaining_days = 0 for the selected leave type. Employee must wait for next accrual or request unpaid leave.
Dates in the pastStart date is before today. HR Admin can submit retroactive requests with a mandatory override reason. Employees cannot.
Overlapping approved leaveAn APPROVED or ACTIVE absence_request already covers the requested date range. Cancel or modify the existing request first.
Notice period conflictEmployee is in an active notice period and the leave type does not allow resumption. Annual leave during notice requires HR Director sign-off.
Sick > 2 days — no certSick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days requires a medical certificate upload (BR-LV-002). System creates PENDING_CERT status and blocks approval until cert is attached.

Approval Inbox Guide
Two paths to the same decision. Every approval screen enforces BR-29 — exactly three buttons: Approve · Reject · Delegate. The 48-hour SLA is tracked in UAE business days via /api/cron/workflow-sla.
Path A — In-App (/approvals)
① Open Approvals in sidebar — badge shows count of pending items
② Filter by Leave tab to see only leave requests
③ Sort by SLA Remaining — red = breach, amber = <8 hours, green = safe
④ Click Approve / Reject / Delegate — decision recorded immediately. Reject requires a reason (min 10 chars).
Path B — Email One-Click JWT
① Receive notification email with leave summary (employee, type, dates, current balance)
② Click Approve / Reject / Delegate link directly in the email body
③ Browser opens a confirmation page — no login required (JWT token auth)
④ Decision recorded instantly. Token is single-use and expires after 7 days.
ℹ If the email link says "already actioned", the request was decided in-app first. Check /approvals → Completed Requests for the full decision trail.

Section 8
Operational Scenarios
Four real-world leave scenarios from AVANIIS Real Estate and Avaniis. Each shows exact resolution steps, compliance references, and what breaks if a step is skipped.
A
Leave overlaps employee notice period
1
Ahmed submits 10-day annual leave from 1 July. HR checks and finds Ahmed handed in resignation — notice period started 28 June.
2
System detects notice period overlap: worker_assignments.notice_start_date ≤ leave start. Surfaces a warning to the manager on the approval screen.
3
Manager has three options: (a) Approve — leave runs during notice, employee returns and continues notice. (b) Reject — leave is not granted; employee serves full notice. (c) Escalate to HR Director — required if annual leave exceeds 5 days during notice.
4
If approved: payroll processes leave pay, then notice pay resumes from return date. Effective end date of employment adjusts accordingly.
5
HR Director signs off at /approvals if leave > 5 days during notice — mandatory second approval step is inserted into the workflow automatically.
⚠ UAE Labour Law: An employer cannot prevent an employee from taking earned annual leave accumulated during the notice period (FDL 33/2021 Art. 43). Rejection must be documented with a valid business reason.
BR-LV-001 · FDL-33-2021 Art.43 · notice_period · worker_assignments
B
Sick leave exceeds 90 days — status escalation
1
Mohammed has been on sick leave for 86 days. ARIA alerts HR Admin: "Mohammed Al-Hassan's sick balance will be exhausted in 4 days."
2
HR Admin navigates to /absence → Mohammed → Sick Leave History to confirm the exact usage: 15 full-pay days + 30 half-pay days + 41 no-pay days = 86 of 90 used.
3
On day 91, system auto-transitions: sick leave is exhausted. HR Admin must choose: (a) Grant unpaid leave extension (HR Director sign-off). (b) Process termination on medical grounds (Legal + Finance approval).
4
If unpaid extension granted: a new absence_request is created with type = UNPAID_LEAVE, linked to the sick leave record for continuity.
5
Medical certificates required throughout. Payroll automatically adjusts to unpaid deduction from day 91.
⚠ UAE Labour Law: Maximum sick leave is 90 days per year (FDL 33/2021 Art. 31) — 15 full pay + 30 half pay + 45 without pay. Exceeding without an extension constitutes an employment risk. HR Director and Legal must be involved in any extension.
BR-LV-002 · FDL-33-2021 Art.31 · absence_balances · sick_days tiered
C
Leave applied after payroll close — retroactive handling
1
Sara was absent for 3 days in May but the leave was only submitted on 5 June — after the May payroll run closed (status = PAID).
2
System detects the retroactive date. Blocks the employee submission but allows HR Admin to submit with an override reason.
3
HR Admin submits backdated leave at /absence → Sara Al-Mansouri → Apply Leave → toggle "Retroactive" → enter override reason ≥ 30 chars.
4
Workflow created: requires HR Director approval for retroactive leave (BR-WF-010). Normal 48h SLA applies.
5
On approval: balance deducted from Sara's account retroactively. Payroll adjustment is queued for the next run as a retro correction item visible at Payroll → Runs → Corrections.
6
Finance reviews the payroll correction before the next run closes. May result in a deduction from June pay if May overpayment occurred.
⚠ Retroactive leave adjustments affect completed payroll runs. Always confirm with Finance before submitting retroactive leave exceeding the current month to avoid double corrections.
BR-WF-010 · absence_requests.start_date · payroll_runs.status = PAID · retroactive correction
D
Manager on leave — setting up team leave delegation
1
Sara Al-Mansouri (IT Manager) is going on annual leave for 15 days. She has a team of 8 direct reports who may submit leave requests during her absence.
2
Sara navigates to Settings → Delegations → Add Rule. She designates Omar Hassan as her delegate for the leave period.
3
Scope: all workflow types during 15-Jun to 30-Jun 2026. Omar will receive all approval emails and in-app notifications directed to Sara.
4
While Sara is on leave, a team member submits annual leave. The system routes the request to Omar (as delegate) — not to Sara. Omar sees it in /approvals with a "Delegated from Sara Al-Mansouri" label.
5
Omar approves or rejects. Decision is recorded in the audit log with both Omar's actor_id and the delegation reference. Sara sees all decisions taken on her behalf in /approvals → Delegation History on return.
⚠ Delegation must be set up BEFORE the manager goes on leave — it cannot be applied retroactively. If no delegation is set and the manager is ACTIVE on leave (status = ON_LEAVE), the request will auto-escalate to HR Admin after 48 hours.
BR-29 · /settings/delegations · workflow_instances.delegated_to · SLA auto-escalation
Section 9
Troubleshooting Knowledge Base
Every problem has three layers: what the user sees, what caused it, and exactly how to fix it. These are the four most common leave support tickets in AVANIIS HR.
🚫 Balance Shows Zero or Is Missing
Annual leave balance shows 0 for a new hire
New hire started mid-month — accrual has not yet run, or the first prorated accrual is less than 1 day
→ Check accrual run logs at /absence → Administration → Accrual History. For new hires, first accrual runs on the 1st of the following month (prorated from hire date).
Balance shows 0 but employee has worked 6 months
Leave rules not configured, or accrual cron has not been running
→ Go to /master-data/leave-rules and verify a rule exists for this employee's leave type + legal entity. Then verify cron health at Settings → System → Cron Status.
Leave type not visible in employee's dropdown
Leave type is inactive or not mapped to the employee's legal entity
→ /master-data/leave-types → edit the leave type → ensure is_active = true and the correct legal entities are selected.
⏸ Employee Cannot Submit Leave Request
"Insufficient balance" error on submit
remaining_days in absence_balances is less than the requested leave duration
→ Check actual balance at /absence → [Employee] → Balance tab. If the balance is wrong, check for a stuck accrual run. If correct, suggest unpaid leave as an alternative.
"Date is in the past" error
Employees cannot submit retroactive requests. Only HR Admin can.
→ HR Admin navigates to /absence → [Employee] → Apply Leave → enable the Retroactive toggle. Requires override reason and triggers HR Director approval workflow.
"Overlapping leave exists" error
An APPROVED or ACTIVE absence_request already covers part of the date range
→ /absence → [Employee] → Leave History to identify the conflicting request. Cancel or modify the conflicting request first, then resubmit.
📧 Manager Not Receiving Leave Notifications
Manager's email inbox shows no leave approval notifications
Email outbox (notification_log) has entries stuck in PENDING or FAILED status
→ Settings → System → Notification Log. Filter by type = LEAVE_APPROVAL. Check for FAILED entries and re-queue from the admin panel. Verify SMTP settings if all emails are failing.
Notifications going to the wrong person
employee.line_manager_id is pointing to an outdated manager assignment
→ /persons/[id] → Assignments → verify line_manager_id is the current manager. If a transfer happened recently, check if the assignment record was updated with the correct effective date.
Manager on leave — no one is receiving approvals
No delegation rule configured for the absent manager
→ HR Admin: Settings → Delegations → Add Rule. Set the manager as delegator, assign a delegate, and specify the date range. Existing pending requests will be re-notified to the delegate within 1 hour.
📅 Public Holidays Not Being Excluded
National Day appears in the working day count for a leave request
The entity-specific holiday calendar has not been configured, or National Day was not added to the calendar for this year
→ /master-data/holidays → select the legal entity → verify 2026 entries include all public holidays. After adding, the system recalculates any existing SUBMITTED requests that include the missed dates.
Holiday calendar is configured but still being counted
The holiday is linked to a different legal entity than the employee's current entity
→ Check /persons/[id] → Assignments → legal_entity_id. Cross-reference with /master-data/holidays → filter by entity. Ensure the holiday record's entity_id matches the employee's entity_id.
Ramadan working hours not reflected in leave calculation
Ramadan schedule is managed via timesheets, not holiday calendar. Leave is calculated in calendar days, not hours.
→ Ramadan hour adjustments are in /timesheets → Schedule → Ramadan Mode. Leave balances are not affected by Ramadan shortened hours — they remain in calendar days per UAE law.

ARIA Intelligence Notes
Eight documented ARIA queries for the Leave & Absence module — each showing the exact dependency-check sequence, the table/field references, and the response ARIA returns. Access ARIA at /aria (HR Admin) or /me/aria (Employee).
ARIA
Adaptive Resource Intelligence Agent — Leave & Absence Dependency Logic
HR Admin access: /aria · Employee access: /me/aria · Manager access: /approvals inline chat
"How many annual leave days does Ahmed have left?"
ARIA checks in order
1.Resolve Ahmed's person_id from persons WHERE name_en ILIKE 'Ahmed%'
2.Query absence_balances WHERE person_id = Ahmed AND leave_type = ANNUAL_LEAVE AND year = current_year
3.Calculate: accrued_days - used_days = remaining_days
4.Check for pending requests (status = SUBMITTED) to show reserved but not yet deducted days
"Ahmed Al-Rashidi has 18.5 annual leave days accrued this year. He has used 6.5 days. He has 5 days pending manager approval. Available to book now: 12 days. Carry-forward cap is 30 days — he is within limit."
absence_balances · absence_requests.status = SUBMITTED · BR-LV-001
"Who is on leave this week?"
ARIA checks in order
1.Query absence_requests WHERE status IN ('APPROVED','ACTIVE') AND start_date <= end_of_week AND end_date >= start_of_week
2.Join to persons to get name_en, name_ar, department_id, line_manager_id
3.Scoped to caller's department or team if caller is MANAGER role
Returns a structured list: "3 employees on leave this week: Sara Al-Mansouri (Annual, returns Mon 23 Jun), Omar Hassan (Sick, expected return 18 Jun), Fatima Al-Zaabi (Unpaid, returns 30 Jun). Team coverage: 8 of 11 available."
absence_requests · persons · worker_assignments.line_manager_id
"Show all pending leave approvals for my team"
ARIA checks in order
1.Identify caller's role: if MANAGER, scope to persons WHERE line_manager_id = caller.person_id
2.Query absence_requests WHERE status = SUBMITTED AND person_id IN (team members)
3.Join workflow_instances to get SLA remaining for each request
4.Sort by SLA_remaining ASC — most urgent first
"You have 3 pending leave approvals. Ahmed (Annual, 5 days, 36 hours left on SLA). Fatima (Unpaid, 10 days, 18 hours — act soon). Mohammed (Paternity, 5 days, 44 hours). Navigate to /approvals to decide."
absence_requests.status=SUBMITTED · workflow_instances · SLA timer · BR-29
"Ahmed wants sick leave — what documents are needed?"
ARIA checks in order
1.Determine requested sick leave duration from context (or ask: "How many days?")
2.If duration <= 2 days: no certificate required — apply leave normally
3.If duration > 2 days: BR-LV-002 applies — medical certificate from a licensed UAE clinic is mandatory
4.Check Ahmed's current sick_days_used to determine pay tier (full/half/no pay)
"For sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days, Ahmed must provide a medical certificate from a licensed UAE clinic or hospital. Upload the certificate at /absence → Ahmed → Sick Leave → Upload Certificate. Without it, the leave stays in PENDING_CERT and cannot be approved."
BR-LV-002 · absence_requests.cert_document_url · FDL-33-2021 Art.31
"Why is Sara's annual leave balance negative?"
ARIA checks in order
1.Query absence_balances WHERE person_id = Sara AND leave_type = ANNUAL_LEAVE
2.Check if used_days exceeds accrued_days (advance leave scenario)
3.Check carry_forward_days — was last year's carry-forward added incorrectly?
4.Check for year-end forfeiture: was excess carry-forward deducted reducing opening balance?
"Sara's balance is -2.5 days because she was granted advance leave in March (approved with override by HR Admin). Her accrual rate is 2.5 days/month. At her current rate she will be back in credit by 1 August. No action required unless she requests further leave before then."
absence_balances.accrued_days · used_days · carry_forward_days · advance_leave flag
"How many sick days has Mohammed taken this year?"
ARIA checks in order
1.Query absence_requests WHERE person_id = Mohammed AND leave_type = SICK_LEAVE AND EXTRACT(year FROM start_date) = current_year AND status IN ('APPROVED','ACTIVE','COMPLETED')
2.SUM(end_date - start_date + 1) for working days
3.Map to pay tier: first 15 = full pay, 16-45 = half pay, 46-90 = no pay
"Mohammed Al-Hassan has taken 23 sick days in 2026. Breakdown: 15 days full pay (Jan–Feb), 8 days half pay (Mar). He has 37 days of half-pay sick leave and 45 days no-pay sick leave remaining before exhaustion. No medical certificate issues on file."
absence_requests · SUM(sick_days) · pay tier calculation · BR-LV-002
"What is the maximum annual leave carry-forward?"
ARIA checks in order
1.Query leave_rules WHERE leave_type = ANNUAL_LEAVE AND legal_entity_id = caller entity
2.Read carry_forward_max_days from the configured rule
3.Cross-check with UAE law minimum: FDL 33/2021 sets 30 calendar days maximum carry-forward
"Under UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 (FDL 33/2021 Art. 29), employees may carry forward up to 30 calendar days of unused annual leave to the following year, subject to employer consent. Any excess above 30 days is forfeited at year-end. AVANIIS HR will automatically warn you when any employee's balance exceeds 25 days."
BR-LV-001 · leave_rules.carry_forward_max_days · FDL-33-2021 Art.29
"Can Fatima take maternity leave next month?"
ARIA checks in order
1.Verify Fatima's gender from persons record (eligibility: female employees only)
2.Check assignment_status = ACTIVE (cannot be on notice period or PENDING_HIRE)
3.Check if Fatima has taken maternity leave in the current year (one instance per pregnancy)
4.Apply BR-LV-003: 60 days total (45 full pay + 15 half pay) — tenure is NOT a condition under FDL 33/2021
"Fatima Al-Zaabi is eligible for maternity leave. She is an active employee with no prior maternity leave this year. Under UAE FDL 33/2021 Art. 30, she is entitled to 60 days: first 45 days at full pay, days 46–60 at half pay. No minimum service requirement applies. HR can submit the request at /absence → Fatima → Apply Leave → Maternity."

Section 1
Module Overview & Preconditions
The AVANIIS HR Approval & Workflow Engine is a configurable multi-step decision system underpinning every HR action that requires authorisation. Every request — from a lateral transfer to a 4-step promotion — follows a strict state machine: one workflow_instance row is created, one approval_steps row per approver is resolved from the template, SLA timers run per UAE business days, and all decisions are immutably written to audit_log. Rejection is always terminal — no reopening. Every approval screen exposes exactly three actions: Approve · Reject · Delegate (BR-29).
✅ Preconditions Checklist — All must be satisfied before any workflow can execute
✓
Workflow templates configured with steps and SLA
Configure at: /settings/workflows
✓
Approver roles assigned to users (MANAGER / HR_ADMIN / HR_DIRECTOR / FINANCE)
Configure at: /users → role assignment
✓
SLA timers set per workflow type (default: 48h, configurable 24–72h)
Configure at: /settings/workflows → SLA
✓
UAE holiday calendar loaded for business-day SLA calculation
Configure at: /master-data/holidays
✓
Email notification templates active and SMTP configured for JWT one-click links
Configure at: /settings/notifications
✓
Delegation rules optionally set before approvers take leave (prevents SLA breach)
Configure at: /settings/delegations
✓
Cron job /api/cron/workflow-sla scheduled and authenticated via CRON_SECRET env var
Configure at: vercel.json → cron schedule 09:00 UTC
🚫 System Blocking Conditions — Workflow stalls or refuses to proceed
No Finance approver assigned
Promotion workflow (4-step) stalls at Step 4 indefinitely — system cannot auto-assign. Fix: go to /users and assign FINANCE role to the correct user before creating the promotion request.
No delegation set when approver is absent
SLA breach occurs after 48h with no action. The workflow escalates to HR Admin notification but cannot self-reassign. Fix: set delegation at /settings/delegations before the approver goes on leave.
Payroll run locked or under approval
Salary-affecting workflows (promotions, acting allowances) are blocked while payroll_runs.status = LOCKED or APPROVAL_PENDING for the affected legal entity. Wait for payroll close before re-submitting.
Effective date already passed — Recall unavailable
RECALLED status is only permitted before the effective_date. Once EXECUTED, the change is live in the org chart and payroll. A new reverse request (e.g. a new transfer back) must be created.

Section 7
Approvals & Workflow Guide
Two approval paths exist in parallel: in-app at /approvals and email one-click JWT. Both update the same approval_steps record. The system does not require login for the email path — JWT is single-use and expires after 7 days.
🖥 Path A — In-App Approval at /approvals
1
Log in at http://localhost:3001. Navigate to Approvals in the left sidebar (all roles with pending items see the badge count).
2
The inbox shows all items where you are the current step approver. Items are sorted by SLA urgency — breached items appear first in red.
3
Click any row to open the detail panel: employee info, effective date, current grade, target grade, salary delta, and business rule references.
4
Click Approve to advance the workflow, Reject (requires reason ≥10 characters per BR-29) to terminate, or Delegate to reassign to another user.
5
Decision is written to approval_steps.status and approval_steps.decided_at with full actor context in audit_log. Next approver is notified immediately.
✉ Path B — Email One-Click JWT Approval
1
When a step is assigned to you, an email is sent immediately with your name, the request summary (employee, type, dates), and three one-click buttons: Approve, Reject, Delegate.
2
Each button contains a signed JWT token. Clicking opens a lightweight confirmation page — no login required. The token identifies you, the action, and the workflow step.
3
Tokens are single-use — once clicked, all three links in that email are invalidated. If you click Approve, the Reject and Delegate links in the same email stop working.
4
Token expiry: 7 days. After expiry, the email link shows an error: "This approval link has expired. Please log in to /approvals to take action."
5
The confirmation page shows the full request details before final submission. The approver can still change their decision on the confirmation page before hitting the final button.

A
Approver on Leave — No Delegate Set, SLA Breach
1
Omar Al-Farsi (Manager) goes on annual leave without setting a delegation. He has 3 pending approvals in his inbox including Ahmed's promotion at Step 1.
2
48 hours pass. The cron job /api/cron/workflow-sla fires at 09:00 UTC and marks all three workflow instances as SLA_BREACHED.
3
ARIA automatically fires an alert to the HR Admin (Mohammed Al-Hassan): "3 approvals have breached SLA — approver Omar Al-Farsi is unreachable." The alert appears in ARIA console and via email.
4
HR Admin navigates to /settings/delegations and creates an emergency delegation: Omar → Fatima Al-Zaabi, scope All Types, effective immediately with end date matching Omar's return date.
5
Fatima receives email notifications for all three pending items. She reviews and approves Ahmed's promotion and the other two requests. The SLA breach is noted in the audit log but the workflows continue.
6
HR Director reviews the SLA breach report in /reports. Omar's return meeting includes a mandatory review of delegation setup best practices per HR policy.
⚠ Prevention: Always set delegation at /settings/delegations before any planned absence. ARIA can be queried: "Who has pending approvals and is going on leave this week?" to catch gaps proactively.

B
Finance Frozen During Payroll Close — Promotion Blocked at Step 4
1
A 4-step promotion for Sara Al-Mansouri is moving through the workflow. Steps 1–3 complete within SLA. The request reaches Step 4 — Finance approval.
2
The payroll run for Avaniis's June period is currently in APPROVAL_PENDING status. The system blocks any salary-affecting changes during this window per platform rule.
3
The Finance approver's email link shows an error: "Payroll for this legal entity is currently locked. Salary-affecting approvals cannot be processed until payroll is finalised." The Finance approver cannot click Approve.
4
HR Admin sets the effective_date of Sara's promotion to the 1st of the following month (after payroll close). This moves the salary change outside the locked period.
5
Once the payroll run reaches COMPLETED status, the Finance approver receives a new notification and approves the promotion. The effective date is still in the future so the system applies the change on that date.
⚠ Compliance note: The SLA clock pauses during payroll lock if the workflow template is configured with freeze_during_payroll=true. Otherwise the SLA continues and a breach may occur. HR Admin should monitor this scenario during month-end.

C
Rejected Workflow — Retry Options (New Request, Not Reopen)
1
Fatima Al-Zaabi's transfer request (Operations → Projects) is rejected at Step 2 (HR Admin) with reason: "Target position headcount is frozen pending Q3 budget approval."
2
The system immediately sets workflow_instance.status = REJECTED (terminal). Fatima receives an email with the rejection reason. The workflow is locked — no further action possible on this request.

Fatima checks the /approvals page — the rejected request shows in the "Completed" filter with status badge REJECTED and the full rejection reason visible.

After Q3 budget is approved (2 weeks later), the headcount freeze on the target position is lifted. HR Admin unfreezes the position at /master-data/positions.

Section 9
Troubleshooting Knowledge Base
Four common workflow support issues encountered by HR Admins and approvers, with root cause analysis and exact resolution steps.
⚠ Problem 1: Request Stuck at Step 2 — No Action for Days
User reports: "Ahmed's promotion has been stuck at Step 2 for 5 days. Nothing is happening."
Root cause: The Step 2 approver (HR Admin) may be absent, unaware, or the notification email went to spam. The SLA cron should have fired but the approver has not responded.
Fix: Go to /approvals → filter by workflow_instance ID. Check approval_steps to see who the Step 2 approver is and their decided_at value (NULL = no action). Navigate to /settings/delegations to create an emergency delegation, or go to the approval row and click Delegate to manually reassign to an active HR Admin.
User reports: "SLA shows breached but the workflow hasn't escalated to anyone."
Root cause: The /api/cron/workflow-sla cron may not be running. Check that vercel.json has the cron schedule and that CRON_SECRET environment variable is set in Vercel dashboard.
Fix: Verify vercel.json cron definition. Go to Vercel dashboard → Functions → Cron Jobs to see last execution time. If CRON_SECRET is missing, add it. Manually trigger the cron via POST /api/cron/workflow-sla with the Bearer token for immediate escalation.
User reports: "The Step 2 approver says they never received an email notification."
Root cause: SMTP misconfiguration, spam filter, or the approver's email is not set in their user record.
Fix: Check /settings/notifications SMTP configuration. Verify the approver's email address at /users → [user] → email field. Use the Resend Notification button on the workflow row in /approvals to generate a fresh email and JWT link.
🔗 Problem 2: Email Approval Link Not Working
Approver reports: "I clicked the Approve link and got 'This link has already been used.'"
Root cause: JWT tokens are single-use. If clicked from a different device or browser after a first successful submission, the token is already consumed.
Fix: No action needed — the approval was already recorded. Go to /approvals to confirm the step status shows APPROVED and the decided_at timestamp is set. The request has advanced to the next step.
Approver reports: "The link says 'This approval link has expired.'"
Root cause: JWT tokens expire after 7 days. Approvers who are away for over a week without delegation will find their email links no longer valid.
Fix: HR Admin navigates to /approvals, finds the workflow row, and clicks Resend Notification. A new email with a fresh 7-day JWT is sent. Alternatively, the approver can log in directly to /approvals and approve in-app — no JWT needed for in-app.
Approver reports: "Clicking the link opens a blank page or shows a server error."
Root cause: The JWT signature is invalid or the signing key (AUTH_SECRET env var) has been rotated since the email was sent.
Fix: Check that AUTH_SECRET in Vercel environment variables has not changed since the email was sent. If rotated, all outstanding JWT tokens are invalidated. HR Admin must resend notifications for all pending items. Use /approvals Resend Notification for each affected item.
⚙ Problem 3: Wrong Approver on a Workflow Step
HR Admin reports: "Step 1 of Ahmed's promotion is assigned to an intern, not his actual line manager."
Root cause: Ahmed's worker_assignments.line_manager_id field points to the wrong person_id, or the workflow template Step 1 is configured to use a static approver instead of the dynamic line_manager_id lookup.
Fix: Go to /persons → Ahmed → Assignments → verify line_manager_id is correct. If wrong, update the assignment (creates new effective-date record). For in-flight requests, use Delegate on the approval row to reassign Step 1 to the correct manager without cancelling the workflow.
HR Admin reports: "Promotions are going to a junior HR Admin instead of the HR Director at Step 3."
Root cause: The Promotion workflow template Step 3 approver role is misconfigured as HR_ADMIN instead of HR_DIRECTOR.
Fix: Go to /settings/workflows → Promotion template → Step 3 → change Approver Role to HR_DIRECTOR. Save. This applies to new requests only — in-flight items retain the original template configuration. For in-flight items, use Delegate to reassign Step 3 to the correct HR Director.
User reports: "No one is set as the Finance approver — Step 4 has no assignee."
Root cause: No user has been assigned the FINANCE role in the system. Step 4 cannot auto-resolve to a Finance approver without at least one active FINANCE-role user.
Fix: Go to /users → find the Finance team member → edit their role to include FINANCE. Return to the stalled workflow and use Resend Notification — the system will now resolve Step 4 to the Finance approver. If no Finance user exists in the org, the HR Director can manually delegate Step 4 to themselves.
↩ Problem 4: Recall Not Available — Already Executed
HR Admin reports: "I want to recall Sara's approved transfer but the Recall button is greyed out."
Root cause: The effective_date has already passed and the system has executed the transfer — worker_assignments has been updated. RECALLED status is only available while effective_date is in the future.
Fix: The executed transfer cannot be undone directly. Create a new Transfer request to move Sara back to her original position. The original transfer remains in audit history. Both transfers will appear in Sara's assignment history with their respective effective dates.
HR Admin reports: "The Recall button is visible but clicking it says 'Insufficient permissions.'"
Root cause: Only HR_ADMIN and HR_DIRECTOR roles can recall approved workflows. MANAGER role cannot recall — they can only approve/reject/delegate at their step. The current user may have MANAGER role only.
Fix: Ask an HR Admin or HR Director to perform the recall. They can access the workflow at /approvals → Completed tab → find the APPROVED workflow → Recall. If the HR Admin cannot see the Recall option, verify their user role includes HR_ADMIN at /users.
User reports: "I thought I recalled the transfer but the org chart still shows the old position."
Root cause: RECALLED sets the workflow back to a pre-execution state — the org chart will not reflect the transfer as it was recalled before execution. But the org chart cache may not have refreshed.
Fix: Hard refresh the /org-chart page (Ctrl+Shift+R or CMD+Shift+R). If the org chart still shows stale data, trigger the org snapshot cron: POST /api/cron/org-snapshot with CRON_SECRET Bearer token. The snapshot updates the cached tree used for performance.
